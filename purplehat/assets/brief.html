01 · How to work

Instructions for the image generator

Goal: 13 consistent images: a cap turnaround (A1), a character sheet (A2) and keyframes K1–K9 for a 30-second anime film. The same woman and the same cap must appear in every image where she is present.

  1. Generate in the order listed on pages 4–5: A1, then A2, then K1 → K9. Do them one at a time in this same conversation.
  2. For each image, use the prompt exactly as written. Each prompt already starts with the shared style line.
  3. Use the images listed under “Attach” as references. A1 and A2 become references once they exist.
  4. 16:9 landscape, highest quality. No text, letters, logos or watermarks anywhere in any image.
  5. The cap must always follow the cap reference on page 3: four curved purple panels with stitched seams, a lavender front visor, and no button or logo on top.
  6. Anu must keep the same face, skin tone, hairstyle and outfit in every image.
  7. Save each result with the file name shown on its card, as the original PNG.

Style

Japanese anime feature-film still, hand-painted backgrounds, cinematic lighting, purple-and-amber palette, 16:9 landscape, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Think of a premium animated feature: painterly skies, soft volumetric light, warm amber highlights against deep purple shadows, gentle film grain. Emotional, quiet and confident, not cute or chibi.

Avoid

  • Text, signage with words, logos and watermarks
  • Extra fingers and warped hands; distorted faces in wide shots
  • Changing the cap's design or colours
  • Neon cyberpunk, 3D or plastic CGI looks, or photo-realism

Character

ANU — an original character: South Indian woman, late twenties, event production lead. Purple PurpleHat cap (exactly as the cap reference, visor forward), black crew t-shirt, wireless headset around her neck, lanyard, rolled sleeves, dark cargo trousers, trainers. Calm under pressure, quiet confident smile.
