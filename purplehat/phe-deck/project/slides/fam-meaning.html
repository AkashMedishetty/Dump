What the visor can say

The visor is the one piece that leads. A small move gives the whole mark a gesture.

10 Tip of the hat

Visor turned 11°: a greeting. For invitations and welcome screens.

11 Forward

Visor moved out 12 units: momentum. For launches and announcements.

12 Lead

Crown recedes, visor lit: Event Management leads the other services.

13 In motion

Each panel turned 9°: a room coming together. The animation's key frame.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 10

These four break a rule on purpose to say something. Tip of the hat breaks symmetry to greet people, which suits invitations. Forward breaks the circle to suggest momentum. Lead dims the crown so the visor, Event Management, stands out. In motion turns each panel slightly; it is not a logo on its own but the starting frame of a logo animation where the panels settle into place.
