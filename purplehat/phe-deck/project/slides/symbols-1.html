Why symbols

A symbol can stand alone on stage screens and merch once people know it. Each is shown with a Manrope wordmark.

Rule: three shapes at most.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 21

S01 Bowler is the simplest and most flexible of all the symbols. S02 Seams and S03 Lens are reductions of the original cap concept, so they keep its story. S04 Eclipse echoes the visor as a crescent. S05 Spotlight is the most event-specific.
