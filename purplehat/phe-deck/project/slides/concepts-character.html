Concepts C25–C30

Character and ceremony

C25

Awards

Three top hats on a winners' podium: awards nights, and being first choice.

Rule: the winner stands in the middle.

C26

Rabbit

Two ears rise from the hat: we pull off the surprise.

Rule: ears in lavender, hat in white.

C27

The Host

A figure in a top hat and bow tie: the host who welcomes every guest.

Rule: head, hat and shoulders only.

C28

Sound Wave

Audio bars that rise into a hat: music, applause and live experiences.

Rule: bars bottom-aligned, round ends.

C29

Lanyard Badge

A conference badge with a hat on it: PCO, delegates and registration.

Rule: the lanyard always shows.

C30

Welcome Door

An arched door on a wide step is also a bowler hat: come in.

Rule: arch, two doors, one wide step.

These give the brand a personality. The Host and the Rabbit are the warmest and most memorable; they would also work as a mascot or sticker set. Welcome Door shares the bowler silhouette with C05 and C16, which shows how strong that simple shape is.
