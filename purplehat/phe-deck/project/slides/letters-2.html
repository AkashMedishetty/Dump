Pairing

Each lettermark is paired with one of the wordmarks: serif with serif, geometric with geometric.

Rule: one weight per mark.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 20

L06 is the purest geometric form. L07 is the most personal and works for a founder-led, boutique feel. L08 is a strong modernist block, very legible small. L09 suits contracts, certificates and invitations. L10 uses transparency and works best on screens.
