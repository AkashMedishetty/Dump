PHE inside the mark

Three ways to put the letters into the cap itself. Each one is for large sizes only.

20 PHE seal

Letters held in the centre opening. Badges, wristbands, lanyards.

21 P · H · E across

Back to front across the cap: P in the back panel, H in the centre, E in the visor.

22 Hidden H

Back panel and visor joined across the centre: H for Hat. A detail for those who look.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 13

These put the initials into the mark. They look strong large, on a lanyard or a stage, but the letters disappear below about 48 pixels, so none of them can replace the plain mark. The Hidden H is the most subtle: it reads as a bar until someone points out the H, which can be a nice story to tell clients.
