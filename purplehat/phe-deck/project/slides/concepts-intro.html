These thirty directions sit alongside the cap mark as alternatives. They are deliberately restrained: a professional events, MICE and corporate firm needs an identity that looks established and trustworthy. Every letter here is drawn from a real typeface, converted to outlines, and every symbol uses no more than three simple shapes.
