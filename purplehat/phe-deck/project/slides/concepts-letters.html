Concepts C07–C12

Letters that carry the hat

C07

PH Ligature

P and H share one stem; the base of the P's bowl is the bar of the H.

Rule: one stroke weight, square ends.

C08

Hat in the H

The bar of the H is a small top hat sitting between the two stems.

Rule: the brim always spans the stems.

C09

P Counter

A heavy P whose hole is a top hat: the hat hides inside the letter.

Rule: the hat sits on the bowl's centre line.

C10

Lowercase ph

A friendly lowercase p and h; the h wears a small top hat.

Rule: round ends, one weight.

C11

Stencil PHE

P, H and E cut from a stencil. The gaps are the seams of the cap.

Rule: every gap 4 units, every stroke 14.

C12

PHE Seal

Initials pressed into a disc, like a wax seal on an invitation.

Rule: letters centred, never outlined.

Letter-based marks are the easiest to own and to read at small sizes. The PH Ligature and the Hat in the H are the strongest: both read as letters first and reveal the hat second. The Stencil PHE links back to the seams of the original cap concept. The Seal suits invitations and premium printed pieces.
