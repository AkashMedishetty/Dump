Concepts C13–C18

The event itself

C13

Curtain Call

Two curtains part, and the opening between them is a top hat.

Rule: the hat is always the empty space.

C14

Audience

Seats in rows make the hat. The crowd is the brand.

Rule: dots on a 12-unit grid, never touching.

C15

Ticket

An event ticket with a top hat printed on the admit side.

Rule: notches and perforation always shown.

C16

Arena

Rows of seats around a stage, seen from the side: also a bowler hat.

Rule: seat rows dashed, stage solid.

C17

Confetti

A hat made of confetti, with a few pieces still flying out.

Rule: pieces only in brand colours.

C18

Venue Pin

A map pin with a top hat for a hole: we find, book and build the venue.

Rule: the hat sits in the pin's head.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 19

These come from the event world: the stage curtain, the audience, the ticket, the arena, the celebration and the venue. Curtain Call and Audience are the most distinctive here: in both, the hat is made by something else, so the mark has a second reading that people enjoy discovering.
