Five wordmarks. W01 is editorial and refined, W02 adds a single italic gesture on Hat, W03 is modern and approachable, W04 contrasts a light and a heavy weight, and W05 is a bold stacked block that works well in square formats. EVENTS is always set in spaced capitals as a quiet second line.
