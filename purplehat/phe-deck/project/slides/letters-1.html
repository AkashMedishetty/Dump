Why lettermarks

A compact sign for app icons, social profiles, stamps and embroidery, paired with a wordmark.

Rule: no ornament, ever.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 19

Lettermarks give the brand a compact sign. L01 is the strongest geometric option and scales down well. L02 is a classic serif monogram, suited to premium print. L03 and L04 are bold and direct, ideal for app icons. L05 links back to the seams of the original cap concept.
