Concepts C01–C06

The hat, reimagined

C01

Spotlight

A top hat made of light: the beam is the crown, the pool of light is the brim.

Rule: the beam always fades upward.

C02

Hat Trick

Three dots rise out of the hat: the magic trick, and three wins in a row.

Rule: always three dots, rising right.

C03

Hats Off

A hat tossed in the air: celebration, and respect for the people in the room.

Rule: tilted 18°, never upright.

C04

Many Hats

We wear many hats: a cap, a bowler and a top hat stacked into one figure.

Rule: three hats, largest at the base.

C05

Bowler

A semicircle and a line: a bowler hat, a sunrise and a domed venue at once.

Rule: the gap is always one stroke wide.

C06

Folded

A top hat folded from flat planes, like paper: crafted and designed by hand.

Rule: only triangles, in three tints.

Six ways to use the hat itself. Spotlight is the most event-specific: a stage light that is also a top hat. Hat Trick and Hats Off come from sayings everyone knows. Many Hats says we do many things. Bowler is the simplest mark of all thirty. Folded suits the design side of the business.
