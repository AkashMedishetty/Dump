Hospitality, technology, culture or luxury: the typeface sets the tone before anyone reads a word.

Rule: EVENTS always secondary.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 18

W06 suits luxury hospitality and corporate dinners. W07 is the most technical and works best on screens and LED walls. W08 is contemporary and friendly without being casual. W09 leans towards arts and culture. W10 is the most traditional and premium, suited to weddings, galas and high-end corporate work.
