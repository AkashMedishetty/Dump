14 One colour

Ink on white. Embroidery, rubber stamps, newspapers, single-colour print.

15 Reversed

White on ink. Dark stages, night events, merchandise on black.

16 Tonal

Three crown tints, visor in white. Breaks the one-tone rule, for large walls only.

PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 11

A strong mark has to work without its colours. In one colour the visor is no longer highlighted, and the mark still reads because of its shape. Tonal is a decorative version for big surfaces such as a stage backdrop; the three tints can stand for the three pairs of services, with the visor in white for Event Management.
