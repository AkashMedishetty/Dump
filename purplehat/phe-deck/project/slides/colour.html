PurpleHat Events · Identity exploration · 24

The palette is deliberately small. Purple is the brand ground, the warm white keeps the crown from looking cold, lavender belongs only to the visor, and ink handles text and single-colour use. The contrast ratios are calculated from the screen colours. Printed colours need to be matched on a physical proof before the guidelines are final.
