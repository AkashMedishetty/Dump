# PhE: basic guidelines (quick reference; the full version is PurpleHat-Brand-Guidelines.pdf)

1. **Logo:** use the supplied files only. Don't stretch, recolour outside the palette, outline, rotate, add effects, rearrange the dots, or retype the wordmark.
2. **Clear space:** two dot diameters (51 units, about 32% of the mark's height) on every side.
3. **Minimum size:**
   - Mark: 120 px or 20 mm wide.
   - Stacked lockup: 160 px or 30 mm.
   - Horizontal lockup: 360 px or 60 mm.
   - Below these, use the favicon or app icon.
4. **Colourways:**
   - Violet strokes with blue dots on Warm White (primary).
   - All violet.
   - White, or Warm White with blue dots, on violet (print and guidelines only).
   - Black or white single-colour for one-colour print.
5. **Colour balance:** about 70% neutral (Warm White or white), 20% Violet and 10% Electric Blue. Blue is the spark, not the room.
6. **Type:** Satoshi (stand-in DM Sans). Headlines Medium with tight tracking, body Regular, labels in Medium caps with wide tracking.
7. **Dots:** at most one accent (Electric Blue) dot per composition, besides the logo's own. Dots are the bullet, the full stop, the status light and the animation hero.
8. **Patterns:** pills, circles and dots built from one module. Nine systems are in the guidelines: Toggle field, Ripple, Stroke·gap·dot, Constellation, Monogram, Lanes, Halftone capsule, Supergraphic and Orbit. Keep patterns out from under text and the logo.
9. **Motion:** fluid, Google-style. Use anticipation, squash and stretch, and overshoot; strokes draw along their centre lines; dots drop, bounce and land on the beat. Videos: flat colour only, with no gradients or glows. The hero animation has no violet backgrounds.
10. **Voice:** warm, clear and quietly confident. "Ideas take shape here." Not "best-in-class synergies".
