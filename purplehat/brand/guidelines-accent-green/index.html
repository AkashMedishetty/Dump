Brand kit

Brand guidelines, business cards, stationery and templates for PurpleHat Events. Everything here is drawn in code from the master logo geometry (brand/logo-geom.js).

Before production: (1) all files are set in DM Sans as a stand-in — swap to Satoshi (Fontshare) in the final artwork; (2) phone, email and website are placeholders; (3) PDFs are RGB vector proofs — convert to CMYK / Pantone with your printer and proof against a swatch book (colour values are approximate).
