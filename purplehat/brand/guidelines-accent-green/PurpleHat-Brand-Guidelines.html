Who we are

PurpleHat Events is a Hyderabad-based events company. We bring people, ideas and places together — and turn the meeting into an experience people remember.

From a 40-person leadership offsite to a 4,000-delegate congress, the job is the same: make it easy for people to connect. The identity is built to say exactly that — open forms, warm colour, and a dot that marks the moment everything comes together.

connected tomorrow.”

01 · Brand

People + Places = Experiences

The whole identity grows from one simple equation. A pill (people, gathering side by side) plus a circle (a place, a point on the map) makes a pill that holds a circle — the switch that turns a meeting into an experience.

The same open, round-ended line draws the logo, the pattern and every graphic device. Nothing is closed off; there is always room for one more.

02 · Logo

The PhE mark

P, h and E drawn with a single monoline stroke and rounded ends, resting on three dots. It is friendly at a glance, precise up close, and simple enough to animate, emboss, neon-bend or cut from a single sheet.

  • Always use the master artwork — never redraw or retype it.
  • The mark can stand alone; the wordmark “PurpleHat Events” is added whenever the audience may not know us yet.
  • Primary colour is PurpleHat Violet on Warm White.

02 · Logo

Construction

One unit drives everything: the stroke width, w. Curves are true arcs, ends are full round caps, and every dot is 1.06 w across — a hair larger than the stroke so it reads as a dot, not a line end.

Stroke1 w
Dot diameter1.06 w
Overall18.29 × 6.66 w
P bowl radius1.9 w
h arch radius1.85 w
E bowl radius1.63 w
Stroke → dot gap≈ 0.3 w

02 · Logo

Why the E has a dot

In the original drawing only the P and the h ended in a dot; the E ran a full bar to the edge. At the client’s request the E’s bottom bar now stops short and finishes on a dot.

  • One rhythm. All three letters now end the same way — stroke, small gap, dot. The gap is 0.3 w under the P and 0.3 w after the E.
  • Three dots, three words. People · Places · Experiences — the concept, counted out on the baseline.
  • A better ending. The mark now finishes on a dot, like a full stop — and gives motion a third beat.
Before — full bar, two dots
After — bar pulled in, E dot added

Type scale & usage

A simple scale with a 1.33 ratio. Headlines in Medium with tight tracking, body in Regular, labels in Medium caps with wide tracking — exactly as on the board.

  • Sentence case for headlines. CAPS only for short labels.
  • Left-aligned, ragged right. Never justify.
  • End statements with a full stop — it is the brand’s dot.
  • System fallback: DM Sans, then Arial.

The dot system

The three dots are the most ownable part of the identity. Lifted out of the mark, a dot becomes a bullet, a separator, a status light, a loader and a full stop.

Keep dots perfectly round and solid. One accent dot per composition is in Pastel Mint; the rest follow the main colour.

03 · Identity system

Motion

The dots are the animation device. Letters stay calm and draw on once; dots do the moving — they drop, bounce, pulse and travel.

Live in the HTML version.
Drop
Dots fall onto the baseline, one after another (P → h → E).
Bounce
On landing a dot squashes, rebounds twice, and settles. Never more than two bounces.
Pulse
A dot breathes 1 → 1.25 → 1 to mark a live moment: recording, “now on stage”, loading.
Travel
A dot slides through a pill — the experience toggle switching on.

Tone of voice

We sound like a good host: warm, clear and quietly confident. We talk to people, not at “attendees”.

01
Warm, not casual
Friendly and welcoming — but always respectful of people’s time.
02
Clear, not clever
Say the useful thing first: what, when, where, what next.
03
Confident, not loud
Specifics over superlatives. Let the work and the numbers speak.
04
Together, not about us
“You” and “we”, people before process. Credit partners.

Your team, one room, one shared moment.We deliver world-class, best-in-class event solutions.
