# PurpleHat Events — three story films (Thai-ad style)

The rules we write to:

- One small human story. The brand stays out of the way until the last ten seconds.
- A turn the audience doesn't see coming. It must be earned by details planted in the first half.
- Very little dialogue, so the films travel across languages: English supers, with Telugu, Hindi or Tamil versions only changing the subtitles.
- End on a line people remember, then the PurpleHat logo animation.

---

## Film 1 — "Small Event" (75 s)

**Logline.** A nine-year-old phones an event company to book "an event" for her grandmother, who is losing her memory.

**Why it works.** An events company usually talks about scale. This film says the opposite, and it is the most shareable idea of the three.

| Time | Picture | Sound / words |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Busy PurpleHat office, late evening. Screens show a stadium stage plan. Phones ring. A crew lead, ANU (30s), purple cap on backwards, eats cold dinner at her desk. | Office hum. |
| 0:06 | Her phone: unknown number. She answers on speaker while typing. | GIRL (V.O., small voice): "Hello… is this the event company?" |
| 0:10 | Anu stops typing. | ANU: "Yes?" GIRL: "I want to book an event. It's for my Ammamma." |
| 0:15 | Anu, half-smiling, picks up a pen. "What kind of event?" | GIRL: "Her wedding." |
| 0:19 | Anu's pen stops. | GIRL: "She keeps asking when it is. She forgets it already happened." |
| 0:25 | Small flat. The girl, MEERA (9), sits on the floor with an old black-and-white wedding photo and a piggy bank. She shakes out coins. | GIRL: "I have four hundred and twelve rupees." |
| 0:31 | Beat. Office. Anu looks across at her team: everyone has stopped and is listening. | Silence. One phone keeps ringing; someone switches it off. |
| 0:35 | MONTAGE, quick and warm: the crew zooms into the old photo (a striped awning, a brass lamp, a specific jasmine pattern). A designer matches the stripe from a 1960s fabric book. Someone tracks down the same film song on a vinyl stall. The lighting tech tests a single warm bulb, not a rig. | A soft piano version of that old film song begins. |
| 0:48 | Sunday morning. The building's terrace. Small, exact recreation of the photo: striped awning, brass lamp, jasmine. Neighbours in their good clothes. | Song continues. |
| 0:53 | AMMAMMA (80s) is led up the stairs by Meera. Confused. Then she sees it. Her face changes. She touches the awning stripe. | Music drops out. Only birds. |
| 0:59 | She turns to Meera and says, clearly, with total certainty: | AMMAMMA: "It's today." |
| 1:02 | Meera nods, crying and smiling. In the background, Anu takes off her purple cap and holds it to her chest. | Song returns, full. |
| 1:06 | SUPER, white on black: "No event is small to the person it's for." | |
| 1:10 | PurpleHat logo animation. "PurpleHat Events". | Final note. |

**Plant and pay-off.** The jasmine pattern, the stripe and the song are all seen in the photo at 0:25 before they're recreated.

**Production.** Live action is best (two days, one office, one terrace). As AI video it is feasible, but faces must hold across shots, so cast one real actor per role or use a consistent-character model.

## Film 2 — "Invisible" (45 s, comedy that turns warm)

**Logline.** A perfect corporate gala, shown twice: first as the guests saw it, then as it actually happened.

**Why it works.** It is funny and fast for Reels, and it sells exactly what clients pay for: problems solved before anyone notices.

| Time | Picture | Sound / words |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Glossy and slow-motion. Guests arrive at a gala. Chandeliers, champagne, a CEO walks on stage to applause. Everything is perfect. | An elegant string waltz. |
| 0:12 | The CEO raises a glass. Freeze frame. | Record scratch. SUPER: "What you saw." |
| 0:14 | REWIND (fast, comic). Same moments, now from backstage at double speed: | Waltz turns into a frantic pizzicato. |
| | The red carpet: two crew lie flat under it holding down a ripped seam. | |
| | Champagne: one crew member sprints through the kitchen with the only bottle opener. | |
| | Chandelier: someone on a ladder screws in the last bulb as the doors open. | |
| | Rain: four crew hold a tarp over the entrance, smiling at guests. | |
| | The CEO's walk-on: a stage manager whispers "three, two…" while holding his shoelace together with gaffer tape. | |
| 0:34 | Back to the freeze frame. Camera pulls back and finds, in the shadows of the wings, the whole crew in purple caps, exhausted, giving each other a silent high-five. | Waltz returns, softer. |
| 0:39 | SUPER: "If we do our job right, you'll never know we were there." | |
| 0:42 | PurpleHat logo animation. | |

**Production.** The easiest to shoot: one hotel ballroom night, two camera speeds. Also the best fit for AI video, because shots are short and gag-based.

## Film 3 — "Seams" (90 s, brand heritage)

**Logline.** A daughter builds a modern events company on what she learned from her father's small tent-house business, and gets to throw one event for him.

**Why it works.** It explains the cap and the seams in the logo without ever saying "logo". It gives the brand a soul for the website and pitch openings.

| Time | Picture | Sound / words |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1990s, sepia-warm. A small-town wedding. A tent-house man, RAJU (40), ties bamboo poles and stretches striped cloth. His daughter (7) holds the rope. He shows her how to stitch two panels together. | RAJU: "A good tent is just good seams." |
| 0:12 | She falls asleep on folded cloth in the back of his tempo at 3 am, the wedding lights still glowing behind. | Distant wedding band. |
| 0:18 | MATCH CUT: the same girl, now 30s, asleep in the back of a car at 3 am after a conference load-out. Laptop on her lap. A purple cap on the seat. | Same band melody, now orchestral. |
| 0:24 | Montage of her company growing: a conference hall, a product launch, an interactive installation with children touching a projected wall. Each time she checks the seams: a stage skirt, a fabric wall, a tent join. | |
| 0:46 | Present day. Raju (70s), now frail, at a small celebration for his retirement in his old town. It's modest; he's happy. | |
| 0:52 | Night falls. He's led outside. A huge tent, made the old way with bamboo and striped cloth, lit like a stadium. Inside: hundreds of people. | Music swells. |
| 1:00 | He doesn't understand. His daughter hands him a guest book. Every page: a couple's name, a date from the 1980s–2000s and "You did our wedding." | |
| 1:10 | Raju looks up. Older couples raise their hands and wave. He covers his mouth. | Music drops to one instrument. |
| 1:15 | He walks to a tent join and checks the seam with his thumb. Looks at his daughter. Nods. | RAJU: "Good seams." |
| 1:20 | SUPER: "Every event is stitched together by people who care." | |
| 1:24 | TOP-DOWN SHOT: her purple cap, its four panels and seams visible, dissolves into the PurpleHat logo. | Final chord. |

**Production.** This is the premium one. Live action with a real period art department is ideal. In AI video, the past and present looks are two separate style passes.

---

## Recommended order

1. **"Invisible"** first: cheapest, fastest, made for Reels, and it shows competence.
2. **"Small Event"** second: the one most likely to be shared.
3. **"Seams"** for the website, pitches and brand launch: the brand's origin story.

# Anime spot — "From Above" (30 s)

**Idea.** The PurpleHat logo *is* the top of a cap seen from above. The film follows the crew lead from setup at golden hour to show night. It ends on a straight-down shot of her cap among a thousand lights, which match-cuts to the logo.

**The character.** ANU, late 20s, production lead. She wears:
- a purple four-panel cap with a lavender visor
- a black crew tee and wireless headset
- a lanyard, rolled sleeves and a clipboard

Calm under pressure, with a quiet smile. She must look the same in every shot, so the character sheet is generated first and every shot is generated from it.

| # | Time | Shot | Image-to-video prompt (motion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Wide, golden hour: an empty venue, trusses half-built. | Slow push-in, dust in sunbeams, crew silhouettes walking. |
| 2 | 0:03 | Close-up: Anu pulls the cap on, visor forward. | She adjusts the visor, looks up, wind moves her hair. |
| 3 | 0:05 | Hands: a cable snaps into a connector; a fabric wall is stitched; a lamp clicks on. Three 1 s inserts. | Macro, crisp, satisfying. |
| 4 | 0:08 | Medium: rain starts, and she calmly signals; the crew raise a canopy in one move. | Rain streaks, canopy unfurls, she doesn't flinch. |
| 5 | 0:12 | Blue hour: the first guests arrive; lanterns lift. | Lanterns rise, slow tilt up. |
| 6 | 0:16 | Backstage: she counts down on the headset, "3… 2…". | Lips move, finger counts, stage light spills onto her face. |
| 7 | 0:19 | Reverse: the stage erupts in light; the crowd's faces glow. | Light wave sweeps over the crowd. |
| 8 | 0:23 | She steps back into the dark, alone, and exhales. Small smile. | Hold; breathing; bokeh lights drift. |
| 9 | 0:25 | Crane up to straight down: her cap among concentric rings of lights. | Smooth rise, rotation slowing to a stop. |
| 10 | 0:28 | Match cut: the cap's four panels become the PurpleHat logo, then the lockup appears. | The logo animation takes over. |

**Production pipeline:**
1. Character sheet and keyframes from an image model with a reference image, so she stays consistent.
2. Image-to-video for each shot with Wan 2.2 (open, Apache-2.0) in ComfyUI on a rented GPU. Kling or Veo also work if you have access. Generate 3–5 takes per shot and keep the best.
3. Upscale to 1080p or 4K, then grade everything to one purple-and-amber look.
4. Edit to music, add sound design, and composite the real logo for the match cut. Image models won't hold the exact seam geometry, so the last cap shot is retouched by hand.

**What the cloud credits would cover.** One GPU machine (L40S, A100 or H100 class) running ComfyUI and Wan 2.2. Rough rule: a 5 s 720p clip takes a few minutes per take on that class of card, so ten shots × four takes is an afternoon of GPU time, not weeks.
