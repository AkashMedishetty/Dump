## What it is

A very popular (about 128k stars) Chinese-origin tool with a WebUI and API. The pipeline: LLM script (many providers) → search keywords → Pexels/Pixabay clips → TTS → subtitles → background music → MoviePy/ffmpeg composite, in vertical or horizontal formats, with batch generation.

## Ideas for us

- Volume social content (tips, facts, event teasers) at near-zero cost, reviewed before posting.
