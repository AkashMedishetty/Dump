## What it is

The most widely used professional web animation library: tweens, nested timelines, eases, ScrollTrigger, SplitText, MorphSVG, DrawSVG, Flip, physics plugins. Timelines can be paused and `seek()`ed, which is why HyperFrames and code-rendered video pipelines use it.

## Ideas for us

- Default choice for kinetic type and choreographed UI motion on installation walls.
- `timeline.seek(t)` makes GSAP scenes renderable frame by frame for reels.
