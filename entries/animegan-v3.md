## What it is

A lightweight GAN for photo-to-anime translation with several pretrained styles (Hayao/Ghibli-like, Shinkai, cute portrait, sketch...). It ships ONNX models, so it is fast enough for event use.

## Ideas for us

- **memory-mosaic / merch:** guest selfies stylised into anime portraits on the spot.
