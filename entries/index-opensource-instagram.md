## What it is

An Instagram account curated by @hyun2xyz (with "Xity" credited for local runs and video). Each reel showcases one open-source project: physics, generative art, procedural generation, computer vision. The first 21 entries in this library came from it.

## How it works (their reel format)

- Vertical video split into **top clip / caption band / bottom clip**: a dark navy band with a bold English line plus a Korean line underneath.
- Opens with "Today's open-source project is X," then one plain-language sentence per beat.
- They **actually run the pinned code** and record the output (no screen grabs of other people's demos).
- The caption ends with a question to drive comments ("What would you draw with this?"), then 📎 source at a pinned commit, ✳ license link, footage credits, and "Curated by".
