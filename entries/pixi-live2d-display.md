## What it is

A PixiJS plugin that loads Cubism 2–4 models, plays motions and expressions, handles hit areas and supports focus/look-at. Parameters (angle, eyes, mouth) can be set from tracking data.

## Ideas for us

- A flat, illustrated anime-style alternative to 3D VRM for avatar-arcade.
