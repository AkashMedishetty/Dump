## What it is

Free (about 1.5k stars) DMX control for moving heads, LEDs and dimmers: a fixture library, scenes, chasers, RGB matrix effects, a show timeline, audio-triggered effects, virtual console panels, web access, and outputs (USB DMX, Art-Net, sACN, OSC, MIDI). QLC+ 5 adds a 3D preview.

## Ideas for us

- The lighting side of rhythm-battle and synesthetic-room: our Node server sends OSC to QLC+, which runs the rig.
