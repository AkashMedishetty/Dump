## What it is

A WebUI pipeline: script → AI illustration or AI video per line (WAN 2.1, Seedream, Seedance, Kling, Nano Banana via ComfyUI, RunningHub or direct APIs) → multi-language TTS → BGM → composite. Recent additions include digital-human talking presenters, image-to-video, motion transfer from a reference video, custom uploaded material, batch jobs and history.
