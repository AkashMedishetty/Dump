## What it is

Packaged software that turns a document into an explainer video with narration, visuals and captions, from a script you approve. Inputs: PDF (scans supported), DOCX, TXT, images, article links or a written prompt.

## How it works

1. Upload the source and pick a style.
2. It writes the narration script (approval checkpoint).
3. It draws original art per slide and animates it in sync with the voice.
4. Edit the result on a timeline.

- **Styles:** 15 in total. A hand-drawn whiteboard style plus 14 motion-graphics styles, including isometric illustration, soft 3D coral, cut-paper collage, ink-wash journal, editorial flat vector, sketch notebook, Bauhaus poster and doodle colour-pop.
- **Output:** MP4 up to 1440p (paid), 16:9 or 9:16, narration in 20+ languages.
- **Speed:** script in a couple of minutes, first narrated slide in about 6 minutes, a typical 8–10 slide video in about 35 minutes.

## Ideas for us
