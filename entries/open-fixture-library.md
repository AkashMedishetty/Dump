## What it is

A website plus repo (about 260 stars) of fixture definitions (channels, modes, capabilities) in a single open JSON schema, with importers and exporters for several lighting programs.

## Ideas for us

- Look up a rented fixture's DMX map quickly when wiring installation lights (rhythm-battle, synesthetic-room) to our software.
