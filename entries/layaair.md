## What it is

A mature TypeScript engine (about 2.2k stars, from China) for games, ads and marketing pieces, digital twins, AR and VR. It renders on WebGL, WebGPU, OpenGL and Vulkan with PBR, Forward+ and clustered lighting. The IDE includes 3D scene, material, particle, blueprint (visual scripting), animation, physics and UI editors, plus built-in AIGC tooling.

## How it works

- Author in LayaAir IDE, script in TypeScript, publish in one click to HTML5, native Android/iOS/Mac/Windows/Linux, and mini-game platforms (WeChat, ByteDance/Douyin, Alipay, OPPO, vivo, Xiaomi).
- Branches: `LayaAir_3.x` are stable releases (default `LayaAir_3.4`); `Master3.0` is development. LayaAir-v1 is the old ActionScript version.

## Ideas for us

- An editor-driven alternative to hand-coded three.js when an installation needs game-like interaction (avatar-arcade, rhythm-battle) and a non-coder needs to tune scenes.
- The route to WeChat and Douyin mini-games for China-facing campaigns.
