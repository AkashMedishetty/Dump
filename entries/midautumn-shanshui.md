## What it is

A small, recent example of exactly the "Chinese art animation by code" genre: mountains, mist and moon are all procedural in one fragment shader, rendered as a 9:16 film, with the soundtrack generated by the ACE-Step music model.

## Ideas for us

- A template for ink-wash reels and projections; combine with the shan-shui-inf aesthetic.
