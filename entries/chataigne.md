## What it is

An artist-friendly "modular machine" (about 1.7k stars): modules for OSC, MIDI, DMX/Art-Net, serial, HTTP, WebSocket, Kinect and many apps (Resolume, Millumin, Ableton...), plus conditions, mappings, a state machine and a sequence timeline.

## Ideas for us

- Event show control: cue the whole installation (TD scenes, lights, sound) from one timeline, with operator overrides.
