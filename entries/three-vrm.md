## What it is

Loads `.vrm` humanoid avatars with standard bone mapping, expressions (blendshapes), look-at, spring bones (hair and cloth jiggle) and MToon toon shading. Avatars can be made free in VRoid Studio.

## Ideas for us

- **avatar-arcade:** each guest picks or generates a VRoid avatar, driven by Kalidokit tracking.
