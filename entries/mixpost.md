## What it is

Unifies social accounts; per-network post versions; conditions like auto-commenting on high-performing posts; a media library with stock integrations; analytics; team permissions. The open-source "Lite" edition is MIT; Pro/Enterprise add more.

## Ideas for us

- A simpler, MIT alternative to Postiz if AGPL is a concern for client deployments.
