## What it is

Desktop software for traditional and digital 2D animation: frame-by-frame vector and raster drawing, scanning and clean-up of paper animation, ink-and-paint, a node-based FX schematic, a plastic (mesh) deformation tool for cut-out rigging, and camera stand.

## Ideas for us

- Hand-animated anime-style sequences for reels and installation intros, with an open toolchain.
