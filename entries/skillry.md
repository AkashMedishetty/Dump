## What it is

A gallery of AI-made videos created with Claude Opus 5.5: 288 motion graphics, 62 explainers, 55 3D scenes and 70 games/interactive pieces. Each video card shows its prompt, creator and tech-stack tags (HyperFrames, Three.js, GSAP, Canvas/SVG/GLSL, Remotion, ElevenLabs, Gemini TTS, Fal). A separate skills directory lists 385 skills for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor.

## How it works

- **Filters:** output type (Web, Slides, Video, Image), role (designer, marketer, founder...) and use case (social content, brand films, product launch films, motion graphics, storyboards...).
- **Video skills** include a "CreatorMotion" family of UI-motion templates (checklists, card counters, chat stacks, roadmap pushes), launch-video styles (Manus 2.0, Cue), and image tools via GPT Image and Seedance.

## Ideas for us

- Mine the gallery's prompts as a starting point for CTX reels, then reproduce the good ones with our own brand.
- "Games / Interactive" (70) is close to our installation work and worth browsing for interaction ideas.
